# MessMate

**Less Waste. Better Meals.** 

MessMate is a dual-interface application built for hostel students and mess committees. By connecting daily menu visibility with real-time opt-outs and meal ratings, MessMate eliminates blind cooking, reduces food waste, and gives committees actionable data to improve food quality.

---

## The Problem

Every day, hostel kitchens prepare food without knowing exactly how many students will actually show up to eat. This leads to several systemic issues:
* **Massive Food Waste:** Up to 30% of prepared meals go uneaten.
* **No Menu Visibility:** Students don't know what's cooking until they walk into the dining hall.
* **Scattered Feedback:** Complaints are lost in WhatsApp groups, leaving the mess committee without structured data to hold the cooking staff accountable.
* **Zero Analytics:** Committees lack the data to know which dishes are universally disliked and causing the most waste.

---

## Features

MessMate bridges the gap between students and the kitchen with two distinct modes:

### For Students 🎓
* **Daily Menu View:** See breakfast, lunch, and dinner menus in advance.
* **One-Tap Opt-Out:** Skip a meal before the mess cutoff time (e.g., 07:30) to inform the kitchen's headcount.
* **Rate & Review:** Submit a 1-5 star rating for dishes after eating.
* **Issue Reporting:** Flag hygiene or food quality complaints directly to the committee.

### For Mess Committees 📋
* **Live Headcount Dashboard:** View exact numbers of expected students vs. opt-outs in real-time.
* **Complaint Inbox:** Track and resolve open student complaints systematically.
* **Data-Driven Insights:** Automatically identify the "Top 3" and "Bottom 3" dishes based on historical ratings to remove high-waste items from the menu.
* **Waste Tracking:** Correlate low-rated dishes with actual kilograms of food waste.
